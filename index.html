How to get there?

By Bike

It’s a cute 30–45 minute bike ride from the city through the old Elbtunnel, harbour, crossing a couple of bridges and riding on the Deich.
We will meet at Landungsbrücken for a communal bike ride at 12:30 and cycle together to Moorburg.

Public transport

The Bus 250 takes about 25minutes to Moorburger Kreuzung which is about 5 minutes walking distance to the place.
